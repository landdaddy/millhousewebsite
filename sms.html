Text Message Program Terms

MILLHOUSE LLC is a licensed residential general contractor in Coolidge, Arizona. This page describes our internal operations text message program.

Who receives messages

One recipient: the owner of MILLHOUSE LLC, on a mobile number he provided himself. This is an internal operations line. No customers, vendors, or members of the public are enrolled, and no number is added without the owner personally configuring it.

How the recipient opts in

The owner opts in by entering his own mobile number into the company's message configuration and texting START to the program number. Consent is given by the account holder for his own number only.

  • Opt in keyword: START
  • Opt out keyword: STOP. Messages stop immediately and a single confirmation is sent.
  • Help keyword: HELP. Returns the contact information below.
  • Message frequency: fewer than 100 messages per month, sent only when a business document arrives or a daily summary is due.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply per the recipient's carrier plan.

What the messages say

Each message reports a business document that arrived, read from the document itself, or a short daily summary. No marketing or promotional content is ever sent.

MILLHOUSE: Travis Heitz sent invoice 5594-2 for $14,700.00, Dillenburg Residence. Filed. Reply STOP to end, HELP for help.
MILLHOUSE: Morning. Yesterday 6 documents came in. Unanswered inbound: 3. Reply STOP to end, HELP for help.

Keyword responses

  • After START: MILLHOUSE operations alerts are on. Reply STOP to end, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply.
  • After STOP: MILLHOUSE operations alerts are off. No further messages will be sent.
  • After HELP: MILLHOUSE LLC operations line. Call 602.901.6044 or email hunter@millhousebuilt.com. Reply STOP to end.

Privacy

No customer or consumer data is collected through this program. The only mobile number in it belongs to the business owner. Numbers and message content are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing, and mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
